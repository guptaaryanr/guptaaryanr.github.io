01
Inverse problems · PDE simulation · 2026

WaveSleuth-Devito

I made this mostly because I wanted to learn Devito properly. You hide a structure in a 2D acoustic medium, put a few sources and receivers around it, and try to recover the geometry from the traces. It grew into a fairly complete little research toy.

Nonlinear dynamics · 2026

Trajectory-Only Structural Regime Detection

I wanted to know whether you could spot a qualitative change in a dynamical system from the trajectories alone. No equations and no answer key. The paper is my attempt to make that precise enough to test.

02 / How I got here

There wasn’t much of a grand plan.

Michigan was a lot of trying things. Embedded systems, computer architecture, circuits, robotics, a nanosatellite project. Engineering physics gave me enough room to keep following whatever I found interesting without pretending it all belonged to one neat specialty.

At Technip I ended up much deeper in computational work than I expected. Most of my time was in carbon capture R&D building molecular simulation and analysis workflows. Later I moved into instrumentation and controls, while also helping start an internal AI initiative across the Americas. They look like separate branches on a résumé. In practice they kept bleeding into each other.

I’m going to Imperial because scientific computing is the part I’ve mostly taught myself sideways through all of this, and I want a year where it is the main subject instead of the thing I learn because a problem forces me to.

03 / Things that keep coming up

A few things I care about.

A

The interesting variable is often the one I cannot measure directly.

That is most of what I like about inverse problems and sensing. The instrument gives you one thing and you have to work backwards to the thing you actually wanted.

B

A model can look good and still be useless.

The VLE work beat this into me. Average error is not much comfort if the part of the surface your solver depends on is nonsense.

C

I want to be able to rerun my own work six months later.

I am fussy about configs, tests, failure cases, and the boring plumbing. If a result depends on me remembering which script I ran, I do not really have it.
